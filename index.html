    Getting started

    Explore a story, one scene at a time.

    Story Atlas maps where characters appear throughout a story. Follow the timeline, compare locations, and trace a character’s journey.

    Read the timeline

    Each section represents a point in the story. The location cards show where characters are, and the character badges identify who is there. Colors help distinguish locations and characters by their associated factions.

    Notes appear alongside a scene when additional context is available. Use the Outline tab to jump to a book or major section.

    Explore characters and places

    Select a character badge or a character in this Field Guide to highlight that character throughout the timeline. Select a location card or a place in the Field Guide to highlight that place.

    You can select multiple characters and places at once. Select an item again to remove just that selection, or use Clear all to reset your selections.

    Make it your own

    Use the Characters and Locations tabs to browse what is currently visible in the timeline. Open Settings to change sorting, show all available characters and places, reveal unchanged places, adjust notes and card widths, or switch to dark mode.

    Use Choose story at the top to switch between available story timelines.

    Tip: Try selecting a character, then a location. The combined highlights make it easier to follow who was where as the story unfolds.
